Visitor statistics: GoatCounter

To see how many people visit and which pages they read, this site uses GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly analytics service. GoatCounter sets no cookies and does not store IP addresses. It does not track you across websites or build profiles. It records the page visited, the referring site, browser and screen size, and the country derived from the IP address, which is then discarded. Legal basis: legitimate interest in basic, anonymous usage statistics (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). See the GoatCounter privacy policy.
